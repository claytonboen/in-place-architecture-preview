Jake Boen

Architect and founder of In Place Architecture, PLLC, in Tucson, Arizona.

From the jobsite to the drawing board

Jake began learning construction as a kid, helping his father remodel their family home. While studying architecture at the University of Arizona, he worked as a union carpenter on bridges in Tucson and as a superintendent on a custom home in Oro Valley.

He founded In Place Architecture in 2009 and established the PLLC in 2014. Today the studio works with homeowners, businesses and other design teams on restorations, additions, new construction and technical documentation.

How the studio works

Jake looks at design, craft, process and technology together. He studies how the space needs to work, how its materials go together and what the builder needs to know. He uses building information modeling to coordinate drawings and building systems when it helps the team make better decisions.

In Place is affiliated with Boen Signature Construction Services, LLC, a separate construction company. Ask the studio about architect-led design-build for your project.
