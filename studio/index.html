Jake Boen / Founder

Architecture is most useful when imagination and practical knowledge work together.

Jake began learning construction at eight years old, following his father through the remodel of their family home. That early education continued through work as a union carpenter and project superintendent while he studied architecture at the University of Arizona.

He founded In Place Architecture in 2009 around a simple conviction: design improves when the architect understands materials, building systems, sequence, and the people responsible for turning a drawing into a place.

Today the studio works across new homes, careful additions, historic properties, commercial interiors, and architect-led design-build. Each project is approached as a particular problem rather than a house style.

Technical fluency

A drawing set should make the building easier to understand.

Jake has worked in Revit and building information modeling since 2002. The value is not the software itself—it is the ability to coordinate architectural intent, building systems, and construction information before conflicts arrive in the field.
