Project / 04

Quail Manor

Tucson, ArizonaResidential / Rebuild

Project story ↓

Premise / 04

Eight hundred sixty square feet made expansive through outdoor rooms, honest materials, and infrastructure that works hard.

Area
860 square feet
Type
Rebuild on existing foundation
Systems
Solar, gray water + rain harvesting
01

Context

The project

A neglected mid-century house was rebuilt on its existing foundation and expanded with a garage, creating a compact home dense with environmental systems and usable outdoor space.

Constraint

Termite damage and years of neglect left little to preserve above the foundation. The new house had to remain exceptionally small while supporting full-time living and a broad environmental agenda.

02 / The design move

The roof was reshaped for solar access. A steel pergola, barn doors, exposed concrete, and a corrugated metal roof establish a direct material language, while storage and technology are integrated throughout.

03 / Result

A 2.5 kW solar array, gray-water plumbing, a 2,500-gallon cistern, LED lighting, passive water harvesting, and home automation turn the modest footprint into a highly capable place to live.
