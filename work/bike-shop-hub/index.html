Bike Shop Hub Headquarters

Tucson, ArizonaCommercial / Tenant improvement

Project story ↓

Premise / 05

A fast, pragmatic headquarters conversion that kept an online retailer moving while rebuilding the systems behind the space.

Type
Former headquarters + showroom
Scope
Tenant improvement + building systems
Constraint
Compressed relocation schedule
01

Context

The project

BikeShopHub.com needed to move its online retail operation and small local showroom into a Tucson headquarters under a compressed schedule. The company later became Campfire Cycling.

Constraint

Electrical and potable water service had been severed, HVAC needed to be resized and replaced, and the company needed to maintain operations through its relocation.

02 / The design move

The design reused existing rooms wherever it made economic sense, concentrating new work on the building systems, permitting, warehouse operation, and a customer-facing showroom.

03 / Result

The resulting headquarters combines showroom, work space, inventory, and fulfillment in a clear plan delivered around the realities of an operating business.
