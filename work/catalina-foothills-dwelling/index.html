Context

The project

In Place Architecture was commissioned to develop the three-dimensional building model, production drawings, and mechanical, electrical, and plumbing design for permitting and construction. William Gansline served as architect of record and developer.

Constraint

The architectural design and multiple technical systems had to be translated into one legible, coordinated set of permit and construction information.

02 / The design move

The work was coordinated in BIM from the outset, allowing the architecture and building systems to be studied together rather than assembled late in the process.

03 / Result

The package carried the project through documentation while preserving clear relationships between the building, terrace, systems, and steep site.
